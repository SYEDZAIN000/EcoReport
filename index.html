How it works

Step 1
Describe the problem

Pick a category, upload a photo and write a line or two about what you saw.

Step 2
Mark the location

Use your current location or drop a pin on the map so the crew knows exactly where to go.

Step 3
Follow the status

The department updates your complaint from pending to resolved, and you can see every change.
